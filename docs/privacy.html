Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 27, 2026 • Version 1.0.0
NoteEdit++ does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal or technical user data.

1. Commitment to Privacy

Your privacy is of paramount importance. NoteEdit++ is engineered as an offline-first, native macOS text and source code editor. We believe that your code, notes, and documents belong strictly to you and should never leave your Mac without your explicit action.

2. Data Collection (None)

In accordance with Apple's App Store Privacy Guidelines:

  • Personal Data: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account details.
  • Document Data: The contents of any files opened, edited, or saved in NoteEdit++ are processed solely in your computer's local memory and storage. No content is ever uploaded to any cloud server.
  • Telemetry & Analytics: NoteEdit++ contains no third-party analytics libraries (such as Google Analytics, Firebase, or Mixpanel) and no crash tracking software.
  • Device Identifiers: We do not collect IDFA, UUID, IP addresses, or MAC addresses.

3. macOS App Sandbox & File Access

NoteEdit++ operates strictly within the official macOS App Sandbox. It can only read and write files that you explicitly select through the native macOS Open (⌘O) and Save (⌘S) dialogs or drag-and-drop into the app window. NoteEdit++ has zero access to unauthorized folders on your system.

4. Network Connections (Zero Outgoing Traffic)

NoteEdit++ performs zero outbound network requests. It operates completely offline and requires no internet connection to format code, highlight syntax, or perform searches.

5. Changes to this Policy

If we ever update NoteEdit++ to introduce optional cloud syncing or online capabilities in the future, this privacy policy will be updated transparently in advance.
